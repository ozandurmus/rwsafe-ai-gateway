Older rows with a non-UUID correlation id are withheld from the masked role. Test: `MaskedRoleLeakProbeTest.seed` ingests the review's cases into `platform`, `surface`, `step`, `reason` and `correlationId`, one batch each: TCKNs, a glued TCKN, a glued mobile, a glued card, a glued IBAN, a lower-case IBAN, a secret, estate hosts and groups, and a folded Turkish name.
